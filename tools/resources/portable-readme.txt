Aerial Atlas for Windows x64

Start
  Extract the entire ZIP, then open AerialAtlas.exe.
  Keep _internal beside the EXE. No Python installation is required.

Your datasets
  Download the survey ZIPs and source TIFFs into images beside the EXE,
  or select Choose download folder in the app. No manual extraction is needed.
  Choose a date under Survey, then Create map once. Later launches reuse the map.
  The selected date is highlighted; each date shows its download/preparation status.
  April's RGB_png folder is adapted automatically; original RGB names stay intact.
  June and August require their input_orthomosaic TIFF as well as the tile ZIPs.
  The datasets are not included. Source image contents are not changed or uploaded.

Explore
  Select a tile ID or enter row_column to show its outline with 1.5x context.
  Drag to pan, scroll to zoom, and press Home to fit the map.
  Settings contains language and appearance options; Help lists all shortcuts.
  Switch surveys at the top. Each survey remembers its view independently.
  Tab focuses the dates; Left/Right changes dates, Enter or Space selects.

Update
  Close the app before replacing it. Keep your images and generated folders.
